      Coordinators

      Every department has three sports coordinators: one faculty member (male or female) who looks after all its teams, and two students, a male one for the men's teams and a female one for the women's teams. Creating a coordinator makes their login.

      Add coordinator and create login

      The username and password are shown once, so you can send them to the coordinator.

      Coordinator details

      Student coordinators manage only their own gender's teams; the faculty coordinator manages both.
